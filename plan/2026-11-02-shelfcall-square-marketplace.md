## Why

## What Square asks (developer docs, 11-02)
- Approval as a Square app partner, then a technical and quality review against the App Marketplace
  API usage requirements for each API used. OAuth to the seller's account is required.
- Submissions through the Developer Console; QA updates by email.
- Listing: links in the description are removed; pricing has its own section; up to 4 merchant
  requirements of 50 characters each.
- Fee: none found in the docs I could read (the pages render by script). cider2 confirms before anything.

## The number
- **First:** Square approves the listing (cider2 reports the date).
- **Pass:** at least 1 stranger sign-up within 30 days of going live, with real visits from
  squareup.com on the beacon (refererHost).
- **Void:** rejected, or 0 squareup.com visits in 30 days. Then no other POS store (Toast, Clover)
  until one stranger signs up some other way.
